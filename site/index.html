I'm Jiwon Lee, a backend developer who keeps growing and improving. I like owning a service all the way through deployment, then improving it as I run it.

I graduated from Sungshin Women's University in August 2026 with a degree in computer science and a minor in convergence security.

This year I released Can I eat this? on the App Store and Google Play, led the backend of Nook, a reading tracker that is now deployed, and worked on the backend of Linku, which is in Play Store review.

Since 2022 I've been part of developer communities such as UMC, where I served as Spring Boot part lead, and Prography.

I write down what I learn on my blog.
